Privacy policy

Convene · last updated 8 October 2026

Convene is a personal app with a single user: the person who built and runs it. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google Calendar data

  • What it accesses: it creates and manages one calendar of its own ("Convene"); it reads the owner's own calendar events and free/busy times to plan around them. It does not modify any other calendar.
  • How it's used: only to write the owner's weekly plan to the Convene calendar and to show the owner what's already scheduled while planning.
  • Where it's kept: on the owner's own private server. Calendar data is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Revoking access: remove Convene at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Convene's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
