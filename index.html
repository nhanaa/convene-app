Convene

A personal fitness, food and kitchen coach. Single user, not a public service.

Convene is a private, self-hosted app its owner built for himself. It keeps a log of workouts, meals and weigh-ins, and turns the week's plan (gym days, cooking nights, commitments) into events on its own "Convene" Google calendar.

There is nothing to sign up for: the app has exactly one user, its owner.
